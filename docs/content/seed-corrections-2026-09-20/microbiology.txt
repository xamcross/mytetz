Microbiology is the scientific study of organisms and agents too small to see with the naked eye, such as bacteria, fungi, single-celled organisms called protozoa, and viruses. These microorganisms are found everywhere—in soil, water, food, and inside our bodies—and they play crucial roles in everything from digesting food to causing disease to recycling nutrients in nature. By studying them, scientists can develop medicines, prevent infections, and understand the hidden microscopic world that shapes our everyday lives.
