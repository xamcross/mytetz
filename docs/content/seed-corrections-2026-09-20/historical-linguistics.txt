Historical linguistics is the study of how languages change over time—in their sounds, word meanings, grammar, and vocabulary—and how different languages are related to one another through common ancestry. Linguists compare related languages to reconstruct a lost "parent" language, such as Proto-Indo-European, and test the method on Latin, the known parent of French, Spanish, and Italian. This field helps explain both why languages that seem very different today (like English and Hindi) actually share deep historical roots, and why languages that started out the same eventually diverge.
